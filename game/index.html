PALMERA BAY

Sun, steel & sirens.

Mara Voss came back to Palmera Bay with one skill the city still pays for: driving. Six jobs stand between her and the Coral Crown — the last score on the bay. Steal cars, lose the heat, earn REP, and the city opens its doors.

HEAT & REP — wanted heat cools into permanent REP that unlocks missions, cars and safehouses. The city remembers you.

WASD move / drive · Mouse aim · Click shoot · E enter & exit car · F steal a car · Shift sprint · Space handbrake drift · P pause · M mute · Spray shop clears heat for cash.

On foot: WASD to run, mouse to aim, click to fire your pistol. Walk up to a car and press E to get in; press F near a parked or traffic car to steal it (the cops notice). In a car: W accelerate, S brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake. Crash into things and the car smokes, then burns. Wanted stars rise when you steal, speed past cops, hit people or shoot. Break line of sight to cool down, or pay at the spray shop in Old Town. Finish missions to earn cash and REP — REP unlocks the next job.
